NinjaHR

An agentic HR platform for Canadian small and mid-sized businesses that runs the whole employee journey - from first job posting to final payout - in one system, with an AI copilot on every screen. Compliance is the feature: Ontario's Bill 149, Quebec's Law 25 and each province's Employment Standards Act are encoded into the product.
