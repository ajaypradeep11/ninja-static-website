Shipped product · Multi-tenant SaaS

Conversy AI dashboard

A no-code platform where small businesses deploy their own AI agents for bookings, billing questions, product Q&A and escalations - through a shareable link or an embeddable chat widget. The app is behind a login, so here are the screens.

RoleHead of technology · full-stack & AI
StackReact SPA · NestJS GraphQL API · Firestore · Gemini 2.5 Pro / 2.0 Flash
ShapeWorkspace → business → agent tenancy, per-business knowledge stores, SSE streaming chat
Linksconversyai.com ↗app (login) ↗
01

Home dashboard

One workspace, many businesses. Each business gets its own dashboard: conversations today, conversion and booking rates, pending escalations, and the agents running for it.

02

Train your agent - step 1 of 4

The no-code wizard. Describe what the agent should do, tick the capabilities (Q&A, booking, payments, budgeting), name it, pick a tone - and optionally add a WhatsApp agent alongside.

03

Knowledge base - import from a website

Point the agent at a URL and it reads the important pages into a per-business knowledge store. PDFs and voice recordings work the same way.

04

Integrations

Per-agent overrides on top of global controls: WhatsApp Business, Stripe, Google Calendar and Gmail connect via OAuth so agents can book, charge and follow up for real.

05

Growth insights

Customer lifecycle from lead to paid, conversion and booking rates, and a growth score built from conversion, sentiment and repeat engagement.
