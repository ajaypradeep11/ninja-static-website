In praise of
getting lost.

There is a particular kind of magic in a day with no shape. A road you didn’t plan to take. A colour you can’t quite name. A silence that feels like coming home.

Read the field note +

We made these worlds as an invitation to pause. To look at the space between one thought and the next, and find that it is already full of possibility.

Here is a small experiment: look away from your screen. Find the furthest point you can see. Stay there for three breaths. You don’t have to go anywhere to arrive somewhere new.
