We're an imaginary floral playground for real-world joy. A place for unexpected colors, slightly unruly shapes, and the belief that a flower can change your whole day.

Inside the bloom room +

Our workshop concept starts with one rule: follow your curiosity. Gather seasonal stems, play with unlikely color pairs, and build a composition that feels like you. This is a design study; workshop booking is not available.

Our approach to flowers +

Less perfect symmetry. More personality. We begin with a striking stem, add generous negative space, and let every twist, bend, and brilliant imperfection belong.
