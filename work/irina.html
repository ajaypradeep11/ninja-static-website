In development · Parametric apparel engineering

IRINA

A workspace for taking a garment from idea to production geometry. Start from a reference image or a blank technical flat, assemble the garment from parametric foundations, adjust measurements and fit, validate, and export files for pattern review and cutter preparation. An uploaded image is evidence, never production geometry - everything is rebuilt from normalized measurements and manufacturing rules.
