Adapted for LocalNinja ECHO ZERO:
- Posed the authored character rig into a crouch.
- Adjusted chin, forward gaze, relaxed hands, and curved ponytail.
- Changed wardrobe to a matte charcoal top, dark denim and ivory footwear.
- Evaluated the rig into a static mesh for a web-based 360-degree viewer.
- Baked color textures, resized maps, repaired pupil materials for glTF,
  and compressed the geometry with Draco.

The character's underlying sculpt, topology, rig, hair, clothing and original
texture work are by Blender Studio/Blender Foundation. ECHO ZERO does not
claim those assets as its own original modeling work.
