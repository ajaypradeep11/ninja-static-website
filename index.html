Custom solutions

How we work

Every custom build follows the same six steps. You know what happens next, what it costs, and what you're holding at the end of each one.

  1. 01

    Talk

    We start with a conversation about your business, what's slowing you down and what "done" looks like. No pitch deck, just questions.

    You getA clear scope

  2. 02

    Quote

    You get a fixed quote with scope, timeline and milestones spelled out, so there are no surprises halfway through.

    You getA signed quote

  3. 03

    Prototype

    A working first version in weeks, not months. You click through real screens instead of reading a spec.

    You getA clickable prototype

  4. 04

    Build

    Full implementation in short cycles. You see progress every week and can steer while it's still cheap to change course.

    You getA working product

  5. 05

    Test

    We test on real devices and real data, fix what we find, and walk you through every flow until you're happy.

    You getYour sign-off

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Launch, hand-over and training for your team. Then we stay on for support and improvements if you want us to.

    You getA live product
